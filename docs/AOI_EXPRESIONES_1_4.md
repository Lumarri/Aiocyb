# Ocho expresiones nuevas de Aoi

Ilustraciones producidas con la herramienta integrada imagegen, usando `assets/aoi/aoi-explain-v1.png` como referencia de identidad y estilo. Los ocho originales anteriores se conservan. Todas las nuevas imágenes son PNG con transparencia y están en `assets/aoi/`.

## Prompt de curiosidad

Use case: identity-preserve. Create ONE new transparent PNG portrait asset of Aoi Bite, the exact SAME adult 25-year-old half-cyborg woman shown in the reference image (reference is identity/style, not an image to overwrite). New expression: CURIOSITY. She tilts her head slightly, raises one eyebrow with bright attentive eyes, mouth slightly open in an inquisitive half-smile, human fingertips resting lightly near her chin. Warm, smart, playful cybersecurity tutor. Maintain identical navy short tousled hair and ivory forelock, amber natural eye at image left, organic teal cybernetic eye and tiny mole at image right, right-image temple implant and left-arm metal at image right, dark matte practical jacket with subtle teal edging, opaque black ribbed shirt. Preserve organic anime linework and hand-painted shading, natural facial asymmetry, warm skin with fine texture, individual loose hair strands, scratched brushed metal and cloth folds. Chest-up, front three-quarter, face large and readable at thumbnail, all hair and shoulders within frame with margin. Actual transparent alpha background, no backdrop, no lettering, no logos, no extra characters. Expression must differ clearly from the reference without changing facial identity. Save the final deliverable as a local image and return its local path.

## Variaciones del mismo prompt

Cada entrada sustituye la descripción de la expresión, conservando las restricciones de identidad, ropa, encuadre y transparencia.

- `assets/aoi/aoi-focused-v1.png`: CONCENTRATION. Eyes narrowed in thoughtful focus, brows gently drawn, lips closed with a determined asymmetric line, human hand holding a plain pencil loosely near her chin. No angry look.
- `assets/aoi/aoi-surprised-v1.png`: SURPRISE OF DISCOVERY. Eyebrows raised, eyes widened with an intelligent spark, lips parted in a small delighted oh, human hand lifted loosely at chest level. Warm discovery rather than fear.
- `assets/aoi/aoi-relieved-v1.png`: RELIEF AFTER UNDERSTANDING AN ERROR. Shoulders dropping as she exhales, softened half-closed eyes, a small lopsided smile, human hand resting on upper chest. Caring but still cheeky.
- `assets/aoi/aoi-playful-v1.png`: PLAYFUL CHALLENGE. Leaning a little toward the viewer, chin slightly lowered, teasing raised eyebrow and a sharp confident half-grin, human index finger lightly pointing toward the viewer. Clever adult flirtatious tutor, fully clothed.
- `assets/aoi/aoi-pouting-v1.png`: THEATRICAL POUT AFTER A FIRST MISTAKE. Slightly puffed cheek, pursed lips with one corner betraying amusement, brows lifted rather than angry, human arms loosely folded at chest. Affectionate comic annoyance, still unmistakably an adult woman.
- `assets/aoi/aoi-blushing-v1.png`: MISCHIEVOUS BLUSH AFTER A STUDENT SUCCESS. A clear natural pink warmth on cheeks and ear, direct coy sidelong eyes, restrained asymmetric smile, human fingertips lightly tucking a loose hair strand. Adult warm playful flirtation, fully clothed.
- `assets/aoi/aoi-celebrating-v1.png`: FINAL COURSE CELEBRATION. Broad proud open smile showing natural teeth, luminous happy eyes, human fist raised next to shoulder in a small victory gesture, shoulders lifted with joy. Meaningful warm celebration rather than confetti or objects.

Curiosidad se guarda en `assets/aoi/aoi-curious-v1.png`. Uso contextual: curiosidad al presentar, concentración al explicar y dar pistas, sorpresa al descubrir un caso, alivio al acertar, reto juguetón al comprobar límites, puchero en el primer error, rubor pícaro al aprobar y celebración al completar el curso. La reincidencia conserva una reacción más mordaz. Sin controles para alternar gestos o diálogos.

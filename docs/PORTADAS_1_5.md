Quiet professional educational mood, beautifully lit tactile ceramic and frosted glass materials, restrained cyan and violet edge light, soft grain, dramatic yet friendly. Landscape 3:2 composition, main objects centered with breathing room for cropping into 16:9. Solid midnight navy background, subtle studio shadows. No people, no writing, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks, no scary hacker imagery. Deliver a finished original course cover, no interface mockup.
